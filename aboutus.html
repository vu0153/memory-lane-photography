About Memory Lane Photo Studio

Two photographers, one calm way of working

Memory Lane Photo Studio is led by Ricky Vu and Mark Lee, two Adelaide-based creatives who value calm direction, natural light and meaningful images.

Our focus Warm, relaxed photography for real moments.

Photography should feel approachable. We guide each session calmly so people can relax before the camera.

Our Story

A bridge between Vietnam and Australia

Ricky and Mark both work in IT, where patience, structure and attention to detail matter every day. Photography lets us use those strengths in a more human way: reading light, guiding people and shaping honest moments.

Ricky brings portrait experience from Vietnam and a warm, people-focused style. Mark brings a local Australian perspective, careful preparation and an eye for quiet details.

Together, we want Memory Lane to feel friendly, accessible and easy to enjoy, without stiff posing or pressure.

Photography for Everyone

Free portraits, simple and human

We regularly aim to create simple free portrait opportunities for individuals in Adelaide: short, friendly and low pressure.

When possible, selected images are shared during the session or later the same day. The only payment we ask for is a smile.
