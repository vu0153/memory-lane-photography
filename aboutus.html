About Memory Lane Photo Studio

Two IT professionals, one shared love for natural photography

Memory Lane Photo Studio is led by Ricky Vu and Mark Lee, two Adelaide-based creatives who bring together technical skill, cultural warmth and a genuine love for meaningful images.

Our focus Warm, relaxed photography for real people and real moments.

We believe photography should feel approachable, human and easy to enjoy. Our sessions are guided calmly, so people can feel comfortable before the camera.

Our Story

A creative bridge between Asia and Australia

Ricky and Mark both work in IT, where attention to detail, problem solving and patience are part of everyday life. Photography gives us a different way to use those same strengths: observing light, understanding people, guiding a moment and turning it into something that feels honest.

Ricky previously photographed in Vietnam, bringing experience with portraits, people-focused storytelling and a gentle visual style shaped by Asian warmth and family connection. Mark lives in Australia and brings a local perspective, creative curiosity and a love for quiet, meaningful details.

Together, we want Memory Lane Photo Studio to feel close, friendly and accessible. Our work is not about stiff posing or making photography feel exclusive. It is about helping people feel comfortable, seen and proud of their own story.

Photography for Everyone

Free portrait moments for real people and real smiles

We regularly aim to create simple free portrait opportunities for individual people in Adelaide. These sessions are relaxed, short and friendly, designed for someone who wants a natural image of themselves without pressure.

When the setup allows, selected images may be shared during the session or later the same day. The only thing we ask in return is the most honest payment of all: a smile.
