Why we do this

Photography should feel open, human and welcoming

Many people wait for a big occasion before having a portrait taken. Others feel nervous in front of the camera, unsure how to pose, or worried that professional photography is not for them.

Our free portrait sessions are a small way to make photography more approachable: relaxed, simple and friendly.

How it works

A short, calm portrait experience

01Join a planned session

We publish the date, time and location when a free portrait opportunity is confirmed.

02One person at a time

The format stays focused on one individual so the experience is quick, fair and relaxed.

03Receive selected images

Where possible, selected images are shared during the session or later the same day.

Who it is for

For one person who wants a simple portrait to keep

This free portrait option is for one individual person, not a full family session, couple session or group photoshoot.

Best suited for: individual portraits, profile photos, personal keepsakes and people who want a gentle first photography experience.
