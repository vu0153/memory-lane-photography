Why we do this

Photography should feel open, human and welcoming

Many people wait for a big occasion before having a portrait taken. Others feel nervous in front of the camera, unsure how to pose, or worried that professional photography is not for them.

Our free portrait sessions are a small way to make photography more approachable. They are designed to be relaxed, simple and friendly, especially for people who may not normally book a professional photo session.

Who it is for

For one person who wants a simple portrait to keep

This free portrait option is for an individual person, not a full family session or group photoshoot. It is ideal if you want a natural portrait for yourself, a profile photo, a personal milestone or simply a small image that feels like you.

If you would like photos with your family, partner or friends, please use our normal family, couple or friends sessions on the main website. The free portrait format stays intentionally small so more people can access it fairly.

Best suited for: individual portraits, simple profile photos, personal keepsakes and people who want a gentle first photography experience.
